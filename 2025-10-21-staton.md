# SHORTCOMINGS OF GENERATIVE AI

## Thoughts and Takeaways from ["These Women Tried to Warn Us About AI"](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-features/women-warnings-ai-danger-risk-before-chatgpt-1234804367/)
If Generative AI is going to be sticking around like it seems it is going to be, it needs some serious upgrades. Readers can see through O'Neil's article in the Rolling Stone that AI is churning out some results that are not very minority friendly. Honestly, it seems to obviously favor results that white men would want to see. Privilege is being inserted into these programs, and people have been concerned as they rightfully should be. 

## Family Parallels with Racism
Racism and undermining ideas are learned and passed down. This is very obvious to see this between parents and children. If a parent holds a certain mindset and are say even somewhat passionate about it, one can surely bet the child will pick it up as well. They are sponges. LLM's are also sponges. If they are the children, then the creators and "AI Trainers" are the parents. If we think in that way for both LLMs and creators as paralled to children and parents, then GenAI as a result of the sponge child and racist parent is in itself the learned outcome that materializes itself as what it has learned. What ever the child (LLM) learns from the parent (creator and source of information) will result in the child holding that belief and state of mind as well (GenAI). The creators feeding the LLMs biased and unrepresentative data will only guarantee that it will provide information that will be biased and unrepresentative.

If we think about GenAI of simplistic terms like family learning, we can see that it really is a problem that they are not being fed more representative and diverse data. The world is already trying to break away from racism and discrimination, but if the creators of GenAI keep feeding them junk and false narratives, they are going to insert that right back into the world and allow these negative ideas to flourish. These language models need to be trained in a more fulfilling and sustainable way.
